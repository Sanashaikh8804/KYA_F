# 13. Startup shape and Y Combinator notes

## The company in one line

Trust infrastructure for AI agents that move money: a bank-side intent firewall that checks every agent payment against what the customer actually authorised, even if the agent has been hijacked.

## Customer, wedge, revenue

| Item | Draft |
|---|---|
| Customers | Fintechs, payment service providers, NBFCs and digital lenders first; banks later |
| Wedge product | Scoring API (agent class, trust score, action, reason codes) + mandate SDK for agent builders |
| Credibility asset | Open benchmark and defence-oriented session generator |
| Revenue | Per-session API pricing; enterprise tiers; later, mandate verification and audit tooling |
| Moat over time | Mandate standard adoption, growing library of attack patterns, shared reputation signals |

## Risks to be honest about

- Card networks and large payment players are building agent credentials; position as the intent-verification and drift layer on top, not a competing token.
- Mandate adoption depends on agent builders; the open SDK helps but it is a real go-to-market dependency.
- Early evidence is synthetic; pilots with real traffic are the next step.

## Y Combinator timeline (checked 9 Nov 2026)

- Winter 2027 on-time deadline was 2 Nov 2026 (passed). YC still considers late applications but cannot promise when it will reply.
- YC runs four batches a year; check ycombinator.com/apply for the next published deadline. Early Decision lets you apply for a later batch now.
- Plan: finish the hackathon prototype and benchmark in December, talk to 10-15 fintechs/PSPs/NBFCs, then apply with a prototype, hackathon result and customer notes.
- Prepare the short application video; submitting a video helps the application.

## Before applying (checklist)

- [ ] Employment contract and IP assignment reviewed
- [ ] Co-founder situation settled (YC prefers builders who can also sell)
- [ ] Degree and relocation plan considered
- [ ] 10+ customer conversations logged with quotes
- [ ] Demo + benchmark public (defence-oriented) after the hackathon's Round 1 is submitted
- [ ] One-page pitch: problem, insight (intent firewall), traction (prototype, benchmark, results), team, ask
